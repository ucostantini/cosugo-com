Naturalization USCIS Civics Test

What is this tool ?

This tool is for you to test your knowledge in American civics. More specifically, the questions used here are those immigrants must answer to during their naturalization interview with USCIS. As an immigrant, you can use this tool to study for the civics test and prepare for your interview. As a general public, you can test your knowledge for fun using this tool.

Why this tool ?

Most websites you will find on the Internet are multiple choice quizzes, which do not reflect the reality. The reality is that the USCIS officer will orally ask you the civics questions, and will not provide you with several options. You will need to state the accurate answer (out of several possible accepted answers). This tool tries to reflect this by making you type in you answer and make it match with the official list of accepted answers, for each question.

This tool uses the last version (2025) of USCIS questions. On the civics test, some answers may change because of elections or appointments. Visit uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates to find any answers that may have changed on the civics test. You must answer the question with the name of the official serving at the time of your naturalization interview.

Official resources

Since this website is NOT official in ANY way and was created purely for fun, as an immigrant, you are encouraged to consult official sources from USCIS.
  • Visit uscis.gov/citizenship-resource-center/naturalization-test-and-study-resources/2025-civics-test to learn more about the 2025 civics test.
  • Visit uscis.gov/citizenship/find-study-materials-and-resources/study-for-the-test to find materials to study for your test.
  • If you are a nerd like me, you can visit the following link to consult the USCIS Policy Manual chapter related to the civics test. Visit uscis.gov/policy-manual/volume-12-part-e-chapter-2

These questions cover important topics about American government and history. The civics test is an oral test and the USCIS officer will ask you to answer up to 20 out of the 128 civics test questions. The 2025 naturalization civics test is an oral test consisting of 20 questions from the list of 128 civics test questions. You must answer 12 questions correctly to pass the 2025 test. You will fail the test if you answer 9 of the 20 questions incorrectly. Officers will stop asking questions when an alien answers 12 questions correctly, or 9 questions incorrectly.

Although USCIS is aware that there may be additional correct answers to the civics questions, applicants are encouraged to respond to the questions using the answers provided below.


65/20 Special Consideration

If you are 65 years old or older and have been living in the United States as a lawful permanent resident of the United States for 20 or more years, you may study just the 20 questions that have been marked with an asterisk (*) found at the end of each question. You may also take the civics test in the language of your choice. The USCIS officer will ask you to answer 10 out of the 20 civics test questions with an asterisk. You must answer at least 6 out of 10 questions (or 60%) correctly to pass the 2025 version of the civics test.
